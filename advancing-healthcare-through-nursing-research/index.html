Advancing Healthcare Through Nursing Research

College of Computing
Thu, 3 Sep 2026 - 12:00
Maya Johnson
Home/ Spotlights/ Advancing Healthcare Through Nursing Research

Dr. Sarah Williams is helping advance nursing research through her work focused on improving healthcare access and patient outcomes in underserved communities.

Working with nursing students and community healthcare partners, she studies ways healthcare professionals can improve preventive care and better support patients facing barriers to medical services.

Her research also creates opportunities for students to participate in faculty-led projects and develop practical research experience before entering the healthcare profession.
