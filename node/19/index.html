Creating More Accessible Digital Experiences

University Communications
Tue, 4 Aug 2026 - 12:00
Emily Carter
Home/ Spotlights/ Creating More Accessible Digital Experiences

Emily Carter works to ensure that university websites and digital communications provide accessible experiences for students, faculty, staff, and visitors.

Her work includes reviewing web content for accessibility, improving page structure, supporting content editors, and helping university departments follow digital accessibility standards.

She believes accessibility should be considered throughout the design and content process rather than treated as a final requirement before publication.
